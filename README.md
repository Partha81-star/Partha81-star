## ✦ The short version

I’m **Parth Omprakash Bhad**, an engineer exploring the space where **AI, computer vision, cybersecurity, networking, and IoT** meet. I like building interfaces that feel almost futuristic: a hand becomes a controller, a sensor becomes a decision, and a messy signal becomes something useful.

### What I’m shipping

- Real-time computer vision and gesture-controlled experiences
- Practical AI/ML experiments with Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, TensorFlow, and PyTorch
- IoT and embedded automation with Arduino and smart sensors
- Secure, well-connected systems backed by CCNA fundamentals

### What I’m growing into

- Advanced deep-learning architectures and applied research
- Human-computer interaction that feels natural, not complicated
- Security-minded product engineering
- Collaborations that turn prototypes into useful products

Real-time hand tracking turns gestures into a non-contact game controller. A compact demonstration of computer vision, spatial reasoning, and playful HCI.
